True Purpose of the Study: This study examined how people's reading comprehension abilities and persistence change when they have access to AI assistance and then must work without it. We were specifically testing whether people rapidly develop dependence on AI tools without fully realizing it, a "gradual disempowerment" effect. We compared performance on reading comprehension questions between participants who had AI assistance for most questions versus those who answered all questions independently. You were randomly assigned to one of these two conditions at the start of the experiment.

Why we didn't fully explain this beforehand: Knowing we were studying AI dependence might have influenced how much you chose to use the AI assistant or how you approached the final problems, which could have affected the validity of our results.

What we measured: Your accuracy, completion times, persistence (give-up behavior), and awareness of your reliance on AI assistance.
